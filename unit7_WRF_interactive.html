Giới Thiệu – Kỹ Thuật Viết Lại Câu

Câu gốc: Reading books is necessary for children's development.

Câu viết lại:

  1. It is necessary for children to read books.
  2. Reading books plays an important part in the development of children.

Mặc dù 3 câu trên sử dụng các cấu trúc ngữ pháp và từ vựng khác nhau, chúng đều truyền đạt thông điệp tương tự nhau.

Luyện tập kỹ năng viết lại câu chính là luyện tập sử dụng ngôn ngữ một cách linh hoạt và làm cho văn phong trở nên đa dạng và thú vị.

Kỹ năng viết lại câu đóng vai trò quan trọng trong việc cải thiện điểm số kỹ năng viết trong bài thi IELTS vì nó giúp người viết:

  • ✅ Tránh lặp từ, lặp ý
  • ✅ Thể hiện được năng lực diễn đạt tốt qua việc sử dụng từ vựng và ngữ pháp một cách linh hoạt
📋
2. Kỹ Thuật Viết Lại Câu – 3 Bước
1Bước 1: Đọc hiểu ý nghĩa của câu gốc.
2Bước 2: Thay một số từ trong câu gốc bằng những từ đồng nghĩa.
3Bước 3: Sử dụng 1 hoặc kết hợp các thủ thuật sau:
  • Đổi cấu trúc câu.
  • Đổi dạng từ trong câu.
  • Sử dụng chủ ngữ giả.
  • Thêm thành phần bổ nghĩa / giải thích nghĩa.
1
Thủ Thuật 2.1 – Đổi Cấu Trúc Câu

Đổi từ chủ động sang bị động hoặc ngược lại. Có thể đảo tân ngữ lên làm chủ ngữ và thay đổi từ vựng để tránh lặp cấu trúc câu gốc.

Câu gốcCâu viết lạiGhi chú
Employers should provide their employees with better salary packages. 1. Employees should be provided with better salary packages by their employers. Đổi sang bị động, đưa Employees lên làm chủ ngữ.
2. Employees should receive better salary packages from their employers. Thay provide bằng receive để đổi cách diễn đạt.
2
Thủ Thuật 2.2 – Đổi Dạng Từ Trong Câu

Đổi động từ thành danh từ hoặc ngược lại. Khi đổi dạng từ, cần thay đổi cấu trúc câu và kiểm tra collocation để dùng cụm từ tự nhiên.

Câu gốcCâu viết lạiGhi chú
The government should support local businesses. The government should provide support for local businesses. Đổi support từ động từ thành danh từ trong cụm provide support for.
3
Thủ Thuật 2.3 – Sử Dụng Chủ Ngữ Giả

Dùng it hoặc there để viết lại các câu có cấu trúc như should, need, must. Cách này giúp câu học thuật và linh hoạt hơn.

Câu gốcCâu viết lạiGhi chú
People need to protect the environment. It is necessary for people to protect the environment. Dùng chủ ngữ giả It thay cho cấu trúc People need to.
Smoking should be restricted in public places. There should be restriction on smoking in public places. Dùng chủ ngữ giả There và đổi động từ bị động thành danh từ restriction.
Lưu ý quan trọng trong IELTS Task 2:
• Cấu trúc: Many people believe that_ → Viết lại: It is believed that_ (Chủ ngữ giả & bị động)
• Cấu trúc: People should_ → Viết lại: It is necessary for people to_ (Chủ ngữ giả)
4
Thủ Thuật 2.4 – Thêm Thành Phần Bổ Nghĩa / Giải Thích Nghĩa

Thêm phần bổ nghĩa hoặc giải thích nghĩa cho một thành phần trong câu gốc, thường bằng mệnh đề quan hệ.

Câu gốcCâu viết lạiGhi chú
The government should support local businesses. The government should support businesses that operate locally. Dùng mệnh đề quan hệ that operate locally để giải thích nghĩa của local.
💡
Áp Dụng Thực Tế – Ví Dụ Hoàn Chỉnh

Đề bài: "Many people say that exercise is key to health while others believe that a balanced diet is more important."

Phân tích vế 1: Many people say that exercise is key to health.
  • Many people say that → It is usually said that (Chủ động → Bị động)
  • Exercise → Physical training hoặc physical activities (Giải thích nghĩa) hoặc Exercising (Đổi dạng từ: Danh từ → Động từ)
  • Key to → Play a vital role in hoặc be an extremely important factor/element (Giải thích nghĩa)
  • Health → Giữ nguyên hoặc đổi sang tính từ healthy và thêm danh từ body hoặc life
→ It is usually said that physical training is the key to a healthy life.
→ Physical training is usually said to be the most vital element in a healthy life.
Phân tích vế 2: others believe that a balanced diet is more important.
  • Sử dụng chủ ngữ giả (it): It is more necessary to have a balanced diet.
  • A balanced diet → Đưa ra định nghĩa: a proper eating habit
→ Physical training is usually said to be the most vital element in a healthy life while others think it is more necessary to have proper eating habits.

PRACTICE

Bài 1 Viết lại những câu sau bằng nhiều cách dựa vào gợi ý

1.
Children should obey the rules their parents and teachers set.
→ Chủ ngữ giả
Gợi ý: It is necessary for children to obey the rules set by their parents and teachers. / It is important that children obey the rules their parents and teachers set.
2.
Governments should spend money on measures to save languages that are used by few speakers.
→ Chủ động → Bị động
Gợi ý: Money should be spent by governments on measures to save languages that are used by few speakers.
→ Chủ ngữ giả
Gợi ý: It is necessary for governments to spend money on measures to save languages that are used by few speakers.
3.
Programs with violent content should be restricted.
→ Bị động → Chủ động
Gợi ý: The government / authorities should restrict programs with violent content.
→ Chủ ngữ giả
Gợi ý: It is necessary to restrict programs with violent content.
→ Chủ ngữ giả & Đổi dạng từ: restricted → restriction
Gợi ý: There should be restriction on programs with violent content.
4.
The environment is being seriously damaged by human activities.
→ Bị động → Chủ động
Gợi ý: Human activities are seriously damaging the environment.
→ Bị động → Chủ động & Đổi dạng từ: damage (v) → damage (n)
Gợi ý: Human activities are causing serious damage to the environment.
5.
The government should carry out regulations to protect endangered animals.
→ Chủ động → Bị động
Gợi ý: Regulations should be carried out by the government to protect endangered animals.
→ Chủ ngữ giả 'it'
Gợi ý: It is necessary for the government to carry out regulations to protect endangered animals.

Bài 2 Viết lại những câu sau (Tự do – Paraphrase)

1.
The government spends too much money on developing space exploration technology.
Gợi ý đáp án: Too much money is spent by the government on developing space exploration technology. / It is argued that the government allocates excessive funding to the development of space exploration technology.
2.
Some unpaid community services should be a compulsory part of high school programs.
Gợi ý đáp án: It is necessary for some unpaid community services to be included as a mandatory component of high school programs. / High school programs should require students to participate in some unpaid community services.
3.
In today's world of advanced science and technology, we still greatly value our artists such as musicians, painters and writers.
Gợi ý đáp án: Despite the rapid advancement of science and technology, artists such as musicians, painters and writers are still highly appreciated in today's world.
4.
Public museums and art galleries will not be needed because people can see historical objects and works of art by using a computer.
Gợi ý đáp án: It is believed that public museums and art galleries will become unnecessary as people are able to view historical objects and works of art through computers.
5.
Too much attention and too many resources are given to the protection of wild animals.
Gợi ý đáp án: The protection of wild animals is receiving excessive attention and resources. / It is argued that too much attention and too many resources are devoted to protecting wild animals.
6.
Artists should receive funding from the government in order for them to continue with their work.
Gợi ý đáp án: The government should provide financial support for artists so that they can continue with their work. / It is necessary for the government to fund artists in order for them to continue their creative work.
7.
The performance of staff can have a significant impact on the success of a company.
Gợi ý đáp án: How well employees perform can significantly affect the success of a company. / Staff performance plays a vital role in determining the success of a company.
8.
Professional workers like doctors, nurses and teachers make a greater contribution to society and should be paid more than sports and entertainment celebrities.
Gợi ý đáp án: It is argued that professional workers such as doctors, nurses and teachers, who contribute more significantly to society, deserve higher salaries than sports and entertainment celebrities.
9.
Wild animals have no place in the 21st century; therefore, protecting them is a waste of money.
Gợi ý đáp án: Some people believe that wild animals are irrelevant in the 21st century; consequently, spending money on their protection is considered unnecessary.
10.
Detailed description of crimes on TV is having a bad effect on society; therefore, it should be restricted.
Gợi ý đáp án: The detailed portrayal of crimes on television is negatively affecting society; as a result, there should be restrictions on such content.

HOMEWORK

Bài 1 Viết lại những câu sau bằng nhiều cách dựa vào gợi ý

1.
Workers should continuously upgrade their professional skills to survive in the competitive job market.
→ Chủ ngữ giả 'It is...'
Gợi ý: It is necessary for workers to continuously upgrade their professional skills to survive in the competitive job market.
2.
Educational institutions should provide financial assistance to students from low-income families.
→ Bị động
Gợi ý: Financial assistance should be provided to students from low-income families by educational institutions.
→ Chủ ngữ giả 'It is...'
Gợi ý: It is necessary for educational institutions to provide financial assistance to students from low-income families.
3.
Online games with highly addictive elements should be banned.
→ Chủ động
Gợi ý: The government / authorities should ban online games with highly addictive elements.
→ Chủ ngữ giả 'It is...'
Gợi ý: It is necessary to ban online games with highly addictive elements.
→ Chủ ngữ giả & Đổi dạng từ: ban (n)
Gợi ý: There should be a ban on online games with highly addictive elements.
4.
Air quality is being severely contaminated by emissions from heavy industries.
→ Chủ động
Gợi ý: Emissions from heavy industries are severely contaminating air quality.
→ Chủ động & Đổi dạng từ: contamination (n)
Gợi ý: Emissions from heavy industries are causing severe contamination of air quality.
5.
The authorities should enforce strict regulations to limit the use of single-use plastics.
→ Bị động
Gợi ý: Strict regulations should be enforced by the authorities to limit the use of single-use plastics.
→ Chủ ngữ giả 'It is...'
Gợi ý: It is necessary for the authorities to enforce strict regulations to limit the use of single-use plastics.

Bài 2 Viết lại những câu sau (Tự do / Paraphrase tự chọn cấu trúc)

1.
The government spends too much money on building infrastructure in metropolitan areas.
Gợi ý đáp án: Too much money is spent by the government on building infrastructure in metropolitan areas. / It is argued that the government allocates excessive funding to infrastructure development in major cities.
2.
Physical education should be a mandatory part of primary school curriculums.
Gợi ý đáp án: It is necessary for physical education to be included as a compulsory subject in primary school curriculums. / Primary school curriculums should require students to participate in physical education.
3.
In today's digital era, people still highly appreciate traditional forms of art such as opera, theater, and ceramics.
Gợi ý đáp án: Despite the rapid development of the digital era, traditional art forms such as opera, theater, and ceramics are still highly valued by people today.
4.
Printed textbooks and brick-and-mortar schools will not be needed because students can absorb knowledge by using online learning platforms.
Gợi ý đáp án: It is believed that printed textbooks and traditional schools will become unnecessary as students are able to acquire knowledge through online learning platforms.
5.
Too much attention and too many resources are given to the development of artificial intelligence.
Gợi ý đáp án: The development of artificial intelligence is receiving excessive attention and resources. / It is argued that too much attention and too many resources are devoted to developing artificial intelligence.
6.
Scientists should receive financial aid from the state in order for them to conduct research on renewable energy sources.
Gợi ý đáp án: The state should provide financial support for scientists so that they can conduct research on renewable energy sources. / It is necessary for the state to fund scientists in order for them to research renewable energy sources.
7.
The behavior of parents can have a significant impact on the psychological development of a child.
Gợi ý đáp án: How parents behave can significantly affect the psychological development of a child. / Parental behavior plays a vital role in shaping the psychological development of children.
8.
Remote jobs make a greater contribution to the reduction of traffic congestion and should be encouraged more than traditional office work.
Gợi ý đáp án: It is argued that remote jobs, which contribute more significantly to reducing traffic congestion, should be promoted over traditional office work.
9.
Traditional handcraft skills have no place in the modern economy; therefore, preserving them is a waste of financial resources.
Gợi ý đáp án: Some people believe that traditional handcraft skills are irrelevant in the modern economy; consequently, spending financial resources on their preservation is considered unnecessary.
10.
Constant exposure to misleading information on social media is having a bad effect on young citizens; therefore, it should be strictly monitored.
Gợi ý đáp án: The constant exposure of young citizens to misleading information on social media is negatively affecting them; as a result, social media content should be strictly monitored.
